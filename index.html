Knee safety

Protect the joint

Avoid for now

Deep squats · lunges · jumping · running

Rule: Muscular effort is fine. Do not deliberately push through knee pain. If a movement hurts: reduce range → reduce intensity → stop that movement if necessary.

Medical review: significant swelling, redness/warmth, severe pain, inability to bear weight, locking, repeated giving-way, or pain following an injury.

Month 1 targetExercise 3 mornings/week without aggravating the knee.Rebuild tolerance first. Fitness comes later.
Rest ~60 sec between sets. Move slowly and comfortably.
