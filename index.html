Knee safety

Pain is a signal, not a challenge

Avoid for now

Deep squats · lunges · jumping · running

Rule: Muscular effort is fine. Do not deliberately push through knee pain. If a movement hurts: reduce range → reduce intensity → stop that movement if necessary.

Medical review

Significant swelling, redness/warmth, severe pain, inability to bear weight, locking, repeated giving-way, or pain following an injury warrants medical assessment.
